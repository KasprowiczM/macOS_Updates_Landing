# Workshop artwork and brand assets

Created 2026-11-13 with the built-in image generation tool. The three scenes are original editorial illustrations, not official Apple photography, screenshots, or recorded update runs. No text, logos, legible metrics, or watermarks were requested.

## Workshop photographs

All scenes use a wide 16:9 composition and are available as optimized WebP at 1672 × 941 and 900 × 507 pixels. The smaller variants are used as responsive sources.

- `public/images/workshop/modern.webp` and `modern-small.webp`: Blue-hour technical workshop, graphite workbench, contemporary silver laptop positioned on the right, generous negative space on the left for page copy. Restrained reflections and realistic materials. No visible brand marks or readable screen text.
- `public/images/workshop/heritage.webp` and `heritage-small.webp`: Matched camera, framing, and workshop environment, with an unbranded 1984-inspired beige CRT computer replacing the modern laptop. The historical machine is illustrative and does not imply product support.
- `public/images/workshop/office.webp` and `office-small.webp`: Bright daylight office with a glass wall and a laptop with espresso on the right, leaving open space on the left for closing copy. Natural light and understated materials, with no logos or readable screen text.

## Brand and social files

`public/favicon.svg` and `public/logo.svg` use the original interrupted update ring and rising arrow geometry. Raster app icons and the social image are also stored under `public/`. Their visual review is part of the redesign verification.

## Design integrity

Manrope Variable and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted. Interface colors, type, radius, and shadows remain CSS tokens in `src/index.css`; the workshop uses one GO blue accent, with amber reserved for HOLD. No external artwork or private machine screenshots are used in these scenes.
